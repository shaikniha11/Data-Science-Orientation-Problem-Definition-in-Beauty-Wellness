# # Beauty Product Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on understanding beauty and cosmetic product performance through data science. It explores how product attributes such as price, category, brand, ingredients, skin type, and cruelty-free status relate to product ratings.
The goal is to define a business problem, formulate research questions and hypotheses, identify relevant data, and propose an analytical approach to support data-driven decision-making in the beauty and wellness industry.

## Business Problem
Beauty brands offer products with different prices, ingredients, categories, and intended uses. Understanding how these attributes are associated with product ratings can help businesses investigate product performance and make better-informed decisions about product positioning, pricing, and marketing strategies.
This project proposes an exploratory analysis of beauty product data to identify meaningful patterns and relationships between product characteristics and ratings.

## Objectives
- Understand the role of data science in the beauty and wellness industry.
- Identify a relevant business problem involving beauty product performance.
- Formulate research questions and testable hypotheses.
- Identify important variables and potential data sources.
- Develop a proposed methodology for data analysis and predictive modeling.
- Identify potential business insights while acknowledging dataset limitations.

## Research Questions
1. Which product categories have the highest average ratings?
2. Which brands have the highest average ratings?
3. Is product price associated with product ratings?
4. Is the number of reviews associated with product ratings?
5. Do average ratings differ across product-target skin types?
6. Are certain main ingredients associated with higher ratings?
7. Do cruelty-free and non-cruelty-free products differ in price or ratings?

## Dataset
**Dataset:** Most Used Beauty and Cosmetic Products 2024
**Source:** [Kaggle Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aashitanoah/most-used-beauty-and-cosmetic-products-2024)
The dataset contains product-level information, including product names, brands, categories, prices, ratings, review counts, skin types, ingredients, and cruelty-free status.
The dataset will be validated before analysis. Its provenance and representativeness must be considered when interpreting findings.

## Proposed Tools and Technologies

- **NumPy:** Numerical computations
- **Pandas:** Data cleaning and manipulation
- **Matplotlib:** Data visualization

## Proposed Methodology
1. Define the business problem and research objectives.
2. Identify and document the data source and relevant variables.
3. Inspect data types, missing values, duplicates, and invalid values.
4. Clean and preprocess the dataset where necessary.
5. Conduct exploratory data analysis using statistical summaries and visualizations.
6. Investigate relationships between product attributes and ratings.
7. Evaluate the suitability of the data for predictive modeling.
8. Document limitations and formulate evidence-based recommendations.
